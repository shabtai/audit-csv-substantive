Top findings

  1. AP-1a / AR-2b / Inventory-4d / R&E-trial-balance (major, recurring) — The General Ledger has only 5 accounts (Sales Revenue, Online Sales, COGS, Travel Expense, Payroll Expense); it has no AP control account, no AR control account, and no inventory control account, and Debits ($1,210,240.47) ≠ Credits ($1,973,281.93). Three subledgers totalling $1.42M (AP) + $2.39M (AR) + $12.84M (Inventory) cannot be reconciled to the GL.
  2. AR-1d (major) — 83.6% of unpaid AR ($1,995,260 / $2,385,726, 493 invoices) sits in the 90+ days-past-due bucket at the balance-sheet date with no allowance booked. Mirrored by AP-1d on the payables side (467 / 543 unpaid invoices, 86%, 90+ days past due).
  3. Inventory-10c / 13d (major) — Inventory turnover ≈ 0.012×/yr (~80 years of supply); annualised GL COGS $159,261.80 against snapshot inventory $12,838,940.73 — pervasive overstock with no allowance for obsolescence.
  4. R&E-1c (major) — GL revenue $1.97M vs sales-tables revenue $7.85M — ~$5.9M gap (only ~24% coverage) means revenue completeness (Objective B) cannot be supported.
  5. R&E-1b (major) — Returns are 24.8% of orders / $1.11M (25.3% of revenue) but no contra-revenue or allowance-for-returns account exists in the GL.
  6. R&E-3 (major) — Top 20 GL entries cluster $4,936–$4,999, just under a $5,000 threshold — approval-bypass / split-payment pattern.
  7. AP-1a / AR g0 / R&E aux (major + moderate, recurring) — Cross-currency Amount is not FX-adjusted (mean ratio across 5 currencies = 1.14× vs ~1.9× expected). Confirms reviewer FAIL g0/schema/narrative_alignment.
  8. AR-1c / AR-1e-5 (moderate) — Zero returns or credit memos across 5,900 sales+AR rows over 30 months; status set restricted to {Open, Partial, Received}.
  9. AR-Terms / AP-4 (moderate, recurring) — All 900 AR invoices carry Net 45 and all 800 AP invoices carry Net 30; zero variance across customers, vendors, and months is implausible.
  10. AP-2a / AR-2f (moderate, recurring) — Vendor and customer concentration: only 5 distinct vendors and 5 distinct customers, each carrying ~$400–525k.

Cross-area patterns

Three structural problems recur across all four area working papers:

  • Disconnected ledgers. The GL is not a control account for any subledger; AP, AR, and Inventory each total to a value with no GL counterpart.
  • No master data. No vendor master, no customer master, no employee master. Many inquiry-only and data-missing rows would be applied tests if these existed.
  • Synthetic ceilings. Ceilings on Amount (~$8K AR, ~$5K AP, ~$5K GL line items) explain otherwise-suspicious clusters and weaken the just-under-threshold finding signal — but the threshold-bunching pattern in R&E #6 still holds.
