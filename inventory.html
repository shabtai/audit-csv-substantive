  • F. Costs follow stated valuation method; inventory at lower of cost or market.

Findings already raised by the enrichment review

These were promoted by the reviewer/trap pipeline and are cited rather than re-derived:

  • SALES DATA__INVENTORY-TRACKING/invalid_email_format/SupplierContact — supplier 'Global Parts' has SupplierContact = 'global parts@example.com' (space in local part). Master-data hygiene issue affecting auto-replenishment, not directly an inventory-valuation issue but relevant to vendor-confirmation procedures.
  • SALES DATA__INVENTORY-TRACKING/semantic_mismatch/ProductName — ProductName is misnamed: 7 distinct values (Phone, Monitor, Laptop, Desk, Chair, Printer, Tablet) functioning as a Product category, not a SKU description. This means the inventory listing has no human-readable per-SKU description — every sub-test that needs SKU identity falls back to ProductID only.
  • g0/schema/narrative_alignment (verdict: FAIL) — reviewer flagged that Amount distributions across the 5 currencies on the GL show a max scale ratio of only 1.1×, contradicting any real multi-currency operation. This caveats every dollar-magnitude finding below: the dataset is synthetic, so the magnitude of the inventory-vs-COGS imbalance is structural, not operational.
  • BALANCE SHEET DATA/cluster_validation/cluster_cfd6dbef (verdict: WARN) — GL is unbalanced: Credit mean 986.6 vs Debit mean 605.1. This is the same root cause as the missing inventory account in 4d — the GL chart of accounts is incomplete, so any reconciliation attempt is blocked from the source side, not the inventory side.
