Audit objectives in scope

  • A. Billings are for the correct amount; revenues are recorded correctly as to account, amount, and period; uncollectible amounts, sales returns, and allowances are adequately provided for.
  • B. All revenues earned during the year have been recorded; recorded revenues represent valid transactions applicable to goods shipped or services rendered.
  • C. Expenses and costs of products/services are valid, complete, and recorded correctly as to account, amount, and period.
  • D. Recorded expenses include costs which are properly allocable to the year and are properly matched with revenues.
  • E. Revenues, costs of revenues, income, and expenses are properly classified in the statement of operations, and adequate disclosures are made in accordance with GAAP.

The single Python script that produced these results is preserved at /tmp/audit_revenue_expense.py (full output at /tmp/audit_revenue_expense.out).
