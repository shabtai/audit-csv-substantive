1 AP-1a A,B,C AP subledger cannot be reconciled to the General Ledger — GL contains no Accounts-Payable control account (only Sales Revenue, Online Sales, COGS, Travel Expense, Payroll Expense). $1,418,925.55 of open AP balance has no GL counterpart. major Establish an AP control account in the chart of accounts and post the subledger to it; reconcile period-end balance before closing.
2 AP-1d A,C 467 of 543 unpaid invoices (86%) are 90+ days past due, totaling $1,246,569 (88% of unpaid AP balance). Aging concentration this severe is implausible for a going-concern AP program. major Investigate whether these are stale obligations, disputed items, or unposted payments; reclassify long-outstanding payables and assess impact on liquidity covenants.
3 AP-1a / AP-7b A,B,C Cross-currency Amount is not FX-adjusted (mean ratio across 5 currencies = 1.14× vs ~1.9× expected). Cites reviewer finding g0/schema/narrative_alignment (FAIL). major Either translate non-functional-currency amounts at period-end FX rates and disclose the convention, or restate the data; the present figures cannot support multi-currency consolidation.
4 AP-4 C All 800 invoices carry identical Net 30 terms; zero variance across 5 vendors and 30 months is implausible for a real vendor population. moderate Re-confirm payment terms with each vendor and update the master file; verify no extended-term arrangements warrant reclassification to notes payable.
5 AP-1c / AP-8c / AP-8e A,C No vendor master table is present; master-data-dependent procedures (related-party screen, vendor name/address match, multi-address detection) are not data-testable. moderate Stand up a vendor master file with TIN, address, banking, and related-party flags; integrate it with AP for ongoing screening.
6 AP-3a / AP-3c / AP-8f / AP-8g A,B Search for unrecorded liabilities cannot be completed: no goods-received indicator, no receiving reports, no disbursement ledger, and no AP-side GL postings. moderate Implement a three-way match (PO / receipt / invoice) and a disbursement ledger; surface goods-received-not-invoiced (GR-NI) at period end.
7 AP-2a A Vendor concentration: only 5 distinct vendors across 800 invoices, each carrying $388K–$485K. Real-world concentration this high carries supply-chain and going-concern risk. moderate Document concentration risk in disclosures; assess supplier substitutability and consider broadening the vendor base.
8 AP-8d A,C One year-end invoice exceeds the 95th percentile: APID AP10102, "Fast Travel" 2024-12-27, $4,853.53 CAD, status Open. minor Vouch to invoice and approval evidence; confirm posting period.
9 AP-8h A 45 invoices fall in [$4,750, $5,000); pattern reflects an artificial $4,995.39 ceiling in the synthetic data, not threshold-evasion behavior. informational No action required — observation reflects synthetic data ceiling.

Assumed balance-sheet date: 2025-06-30 (latest GL TxnDate). The dataset is multi-year (2023-01-01 to 2025-06-30) and contains no explicit period closure marker.

Audit objectives in scope

  • A. Accounts payable reflected in the balance sheet represent authentic obligations and include unprocessed invoices and liabilities for goods/services received but not billed.
  • B. Accounts payable amounts agree with invoices or other supporting documents and are recorded correctly as to account and period.
  • C. Accounts payable are properly classified as current liabilities; significant debit balances are re-classified to other assets; and adequate disclosures are made for related-party AP, AP with specified payment terms, and assets pledged as collateral.

Cross-cutting reviewer evidence cited

The AP listing's Amount column is therefore not currency-adjusted; any ratio or magnitude analysis built on raw Amount is unreliable until either FX rates are applied or the engagement reverts to a single-currency presentation.
