Audit objectives in scope

  • A. Receivables reflected in the balance sheet exist, are for valid transactions, and include all authentic obligations of third parties to the entity.
  • B. Billings are for the correct amount and uncollectible accounts are promptly identified and provided for. The allowance for uncollectible accounts is adequate.
  • C. Receivables are properly classified between current and noncurrent assets and disclosures are adequate with respect to assigned, pledged, unbilled, discounted and related-party receivables, and transfers of receivables.

Cross-cutting findings (synthetic-data signals)

The AR file passes its internal arithmetic but the shape of the data is the audit signal:

  1. Currency-amount independence (g0 narrative-alignment fail). Median ratio across AUD/CAD/EUR/GBP/USD = 1.14x. Real-world FX would produce ≥ 1.5x for these pairs. AR Amount is unit-blind.
  2. Term homogeneity. All 900 AR invoices have Terms = "Net 45" and DueDate − InvoiceDate = 45 days exactly. No special-terms clients, no custom credit periods. Implausible for a 5-customer wholesale book.
  3. Status alphabet. {Open, Partial, Received} only — no Cancelled, Disputed, WrittenOff, RefundIssued. No "Paid" status either; "Received" is the lifecycle terminal state.
  4. Zero returns / credit memos across 5,900 sales rows over 30 months and 0 negative AR amounts. Combined with absence of any allowance / write-off stream means the engagement has no ability to test allowance adequacy from data alone (AR-1e-3, -4; AR-10b).
  5. Amount cap. 99th-pct = $7,954; max = $7,996 — distribution suggests a synthetic ceiling near $8,000 rather than long-tailed real-world invoices.
  6. No GL AR control account. GL chart contains only Travel Expense, Online Sales, Sales Revenue, COGS, Payroll Expense. AR-listing-to-GL reconciliation (AR-2b) is structurally impossible.
  7. 90+ bucket dominance. $1,995,260 of $2,385,726 (83.6%) of unpaid AR is in the 90+ bucket. If real, this is a going-concern signal; given (1)-(6) it is more likely a synthetic-data artifact (no payment-collection model in the data generator).
  8. Customer concentration. Only 5 customer names total; Open+Partial is roughly evenly distributed ($445k–$525k each). No long tail.
